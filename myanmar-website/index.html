Introduction

People, land & faith

Myanmar (also called Burma) lies in Southeast Asia, bordering China, India, Bangladesh, Laos and Thailand, with a long coastline.

People & language

About 55 million people live in Myanmar, from 135 officially recognised ethnic groups including the Bamar, Shan, Karen, Rakhine, Mon, Kachin, Chin and Kayah. Burmese is the national language.

Magnificent landscape

The Irrawaddy River flows from north to south. There are highlands in the north and east, Inle Lake, dry central plains and a long coast with the Mergui Archipelago.

Culture & food

People wear the longyi wrap skirt and paint thanaka paste on their faces. Marionette puppets, lacquerware and mohinga fish noodle soup are loved traditions. Thingyan, the April water festival, welcomes the new year.

Religion

About 88% follow Theravada Buddhism. Christianity, Islam and Hinduism are also practised, and many people honour nat spirits. Take off shoes and socks at pagodas and dress modestly.

A short history

Pyu city-states · c. 200 BCE–900 CEEarly Buddhist city-states. The ancient Pyu cities are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Bagan Kingdom · 849–1297King Anawrahta unified the land and thousands of temples rose on the Bagan plain, listed by UNESCO in 2019.
Taungoo & Konbaung dynasties · 1500s–1885Powerful kingdoms ruled from Bago, Ava, Amarapura and finally Mandalay.
Colonial era · 1824–1948Britain gradually took control. Independence came on 4 January 1948.
Modern Myanmar · 1948–todayA long period of political change and conflict. Many communities are working toward peace and recovery.

Travel guide

Places, transport & stay

Everything you need to know before planning. For costs, use the calculator above.

Places of interest

Shwedagon Pagoda in Yangon, the temples of Bagan, Inle Lake, Mandalay and U Bein Bridge, Ngapali Beach, the Golden Rock (Kyaiktiyo), Mrauk-U and the karst mountains of Hpa-An.

Transport

Domestic flights link Yangon, Bagan, Mandalay, Inle (Heho airport) and Ngapali. Long-distance buses and trains are cheaper but slower, and river boats are another option. In towns use taxis or motorbike taxis, and rent e-bikes in Bagan.

Accommodation

Guesthouses cost about $15–30, comfortable hotels $50–110, and boutique hotels and resorts from $150. Many hotels in Bagan, Inle and Ngapali include breakfast.

Best time to visit

November to February is cool and dry, the best season. March to May is very hot. June to October is the rainy season, with green scenery and fewer visitors.

Money & connectivity

The local currency is the kyat. Bring clean, crisp US dollars as a backup, as cards are limited outside big hotels. A local SIM card is easy to buy.

Before you go

Because of ongoing conflict, safety varies greatly by region and changes quickly. Check your government's latest travel advice, avoid unsafe areas, and confirm visa rules and flights.
