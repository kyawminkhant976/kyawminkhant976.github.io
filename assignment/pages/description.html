Description

Early Life

Kai Cenat was born on 16 December 2001 in New York and grew up in the Bronx. He attended Bronx Community College before focusing full-time on creating content online.

Career

Kai began streaming in 2018 and built a large audience on Twitch and YouTube with his high-energy personality, comedy, gaming, and collaborations with other creators and celebrities. He is known for his long-form subathon events called "Mafiathon," which set records for subscribers on Twitch. His second Mafiathon in 2023 broke the platform's subscriber record, and Mafiathon 3 in 2025 went even further, reaching over 1 million subscribers and becoming the most-subscribed stream in Twitch history. He has also grown a large YouTube following with vlogs, challenges, and collab videos.
